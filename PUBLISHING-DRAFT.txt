Habit Garden — publishing and marketing plan (proposal, not executed)

Audience: people who want a simple private daily habit checklist.
Value proposition: turn a small daily action into visible progress without a cloud account.
Store draft: "Grow your daily habits with a simple checklist, progress history, a fresh quote and optional local reminders. Your garden is stored on your device."

Before release:
1. Test login, account isolation, persistence, accessibility, back navigation and notification permission/receipt on Android and iOS devices.
2. Review local prototype authentication and decide whether to remove account passwords or implement a production backend.
3. Add automated regression tests, data export/recovery and a documented storage migration policy.
4. Audit dependency licenses, implement a real privacy policy describing local data and requests to DummyJSON, and prepare accurate screenshots.
5. Create a public source repository only after reviewing files for secrets and approving publication.
6. Configure unique application identifiers, icons, release signing and developer accounts; review current store requirements and costs before any purchase or submission.
7. Run a small pilot, collect voluntary feedback, fix issues, then propose a release.

Marketing proposal: short demo showing a habit added, completed and restored after reopening; clear website copy; no claims of health benefits, artificial intelligence or cloud synchronization.
